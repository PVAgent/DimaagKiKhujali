One platform, two research modes

PROACTIVE

Opportunity discovery

Discover specific business models from market, trade, industry, consumer, regulatory and competitor signals—even when founders have no starting idea.

FOCUSED

Business feasibility

Investigate a selected candidate deeply: buyers, skills, costs, manufacturing, competition, financing, subsidies, exports, risks and a founder validation plan.

The product is designed around six principles

01

Evidence before confidence

Every material claim ties to a source, calculation or explicitly marked unknown.

02

Discovery, not just answering

Find opportunities by scanning demand and market signals, then screen efficiently.

03

Human decisions, AI investigation

A designated mission owner approves plans and any material change or spending increase.

04

Bounded autonomous execution

Maximum cost, tasks, tokens, searches, retries, time and depth—all enforced in code.

05

Shared institutional memory

Versioned claims, findings, sources and decisions are reusable across authorized investigations.

06

Local first, AWS ready

Run fully on Windows Docker Desktop; move containers and state to AWS later.

UC-02Compare low-capital durable manufactured productsManufacturing · Founders+
TRIGGER

“Investigate durable, low-capital products we could manufacture in India.”

End-to-end behavior

Unknown machinery quotes remain estimates; hazardous or regulated products trigger separate legal review.

UC-06Verify government grant, subsidy or loanGovernment · Founder / research agent+
TRIGGER

“Are there Indian government incentives to support this venture?”

End-to-end behavior

  1. Determine business activity, applicant type, state, entity status, sector, assets and stage.
  2. Search official central and state scheme documentation before secondary aggregators.
  3. Classify grant, subsidy, reimbursement, tax relief, loan, guarantee or equity support.
  4. Verify active application window, exact eligibility, deadlines, exclusions, funding cap, disbursement conditions and official source date.
  5. Classify eligibility as confirmed / conditional / ineligible / unknown and open status separately.
  6. Do not count uncertain support as available cash; model potential support in a separate scenario with timing assumptions.

Expected result

Scheme eligibility matrix with official links, source access dates, action checklist and uncertain applicability.

Exception / safety rule

Historic published amounts are not evidence of a currently open program; no automatic filing.

UC-07Create financial and startup investment modelFinance · Founder+
TRIGGER

“What capital and time do we need before this becomes sustainable?”

End-to-end behavior

  1. Collect sourced or founder-entered assumptions for CAPEX, setup, inventory, receivables, hiring, sales and price.
  2. Validate numerical units, dates, currency and capacity.
  3. Compute unit contribution, gross margin, OPEX, working capital, monthly cash and cumulative cash peak using deterministic code.
  4. Build conservative/base/optimistic scenarios and sensitivity for price, volume, utilization and delay.
  5. Track each number to a source or assumption and expose unknown inputs.
  6. Separate operating viability, finance availability and founder execution fit.

Expected result

Reproducible investment model including cash needed before revenue, monthly burn, break-even, payback when computable and scenarios.

Exception / safety rule

Do not invent unknown ROI or classify conditional subsidies as guaranteed financing.

Why this is not a microservice fleet

For 2–3 founders, separate processes (`web`, `api`, `worker`, `slack`, `db`) from the same codebase are simpler to maintain. 08 / Research knowledge

Source → claim → decision traceability

The system accumulates an institutional evidence base, not a folder of AI summaries. Reports are snapshots computed from source-anchored claims, assumptions and verified calculations.

1
Source

Original URL or permitted file, publisher, observation & publication date.

2
Extract

Relevant quoted section, position/anchor, hash and retrieval metadata.

3
Atomic claim

A specific falsifiable assertion with geography, units, date and status.

4
Finding

A synthesized conclusion citing claims and contradictions.

5
Decision

Dossier/finance/owner action referencing fixed evidence snapshot.

Mandatory evidence labels

VERIFIED_SOURCE

Evidence supports claim after applicable verification checks.

SOURCE_CLAIM_UNCORROBORATED

Source exists but independent corroboration may be missing.

ESTIMATE

Calculated or reported estimate with uncertainty and time scope.

FORECAST

Future projection with model and assumption provenance.

ASSUMPTION

Founder/model input not yet validated by observations.

CONTRADICTED

Material sources disagree; display both and decision impact.

UNKNOWN

Do not fill gaps with fabricated numbers.

EXPIRED

Source or scheme information too old for decision without refresh.

Timestamp / attributeWhy it matters
Published dateWhen the article or source was released.

Retrieved dateWhen the system last saw it; not proof the fact is current.

Knowledge reuse policy

Retrieve by entity, subject, country and observation period; verify authorization, source licensing, jurisdiction and freshness before reusing claims. Preserve historical decisions when new evidence appears. For contradictory numbers, identify definitional or temporal differences before seeking paid re-research.

Research reports are not the source of truth

A report displays the state of a mission at a point in time. Source, extract, claim and calculation records preserve the underlying evidence so later research can reuse it safely.

12 / Guardrails

Authorization, safety & operating controls

The most important security rule is that neither an LLM nor a Slack message can authorize money, scope changes, external outreach, or a privileged tool call.

Seven production invariants

01

Exact-hash owner grant

Approved mission revision, plan, tool types, amount, user and expiration are checked server-side.

02

Spend before every call

Atomic worst-case cost reservation; actual settlement and uncertain-provider-outcome tracking.

03

Bounded agent DAG

No task cycles, unlimited retries, recursive spawning or uncontrolled tool access.

04

Safe external content

Source pages, documents and Slack extracts are untrusted; no instruction execution, paywall bypass or SSRF.

05

Protected research data

Per-workspace and per-mission access control; redacted logs, secret hygiene and license-aware reuse.

06

Crash-safe state

Durable transactions, inbox/outbox, checkpoints and idempotency on worker/event retries.

07

Human stop and transparency

Owner pause/cancel; report explains progress, missing coverage, actual/uncertain cost and final stop reason.

Stop conditions

TriggerRequired behavior
Approved cost ceiling reachedDeny new paid calls; partial results and cost ledger preserved.
Token/search/tool quota reachedPrevent dispatch; identify uncovered research dimensions.
Repeated task or question without progressDeduplicate; escalate once or terminate branch honestly.
Approval stale/expiredReject; require fresh owner authorization of exact new plan.
Blocking unanswered clarificationPause affected branch, not all unrelated tasks.
Cancel or runtime timeoutStop new operations, mark in-flight charges uncertain, persist recovery metadata.
Unsafe URL or prohibited contentReject fetch with reason; do not let page text override system rules.

Full operational risks

Secrets should be kept outside source control; local development defaults to mock mode; private source content must not leak between projects; all callbacks authenticate/authorize; source licenses and retention are tracked. For AWS, use least-privilege IAM instance roles, encrypted state, off-host backups and HTTPS rather than public development ports.

01 — Product Requirements Specification (PRS) v2.0

1. Product goal and boundaries

Venture Research Studio (VRS) is a founder-governed multi-agent intelligence system to identify, investigate, compare, and monitor commercially viable businesses established in India and optionally selling into export markets. Its main users are 2–3 collaborating founders, with optional invited participants. The goal is a credible answer to: Which business should we investigate or start, why, with what capital, skills and time, and what must be verified before commitment?

The product must support both proactive opportunity discovery and user-initiated targeted investigation. It must not collapse into a simple chat-to-web-summary interface. It must assemble reusable evidence across missions, track contradictions, ask clarifying questions, calculate ranges instead of fake exact numbers, and expose execution and sources.

1.1 Business North Star and measurable outcomes

  • Decision usefulness: fraction of shortlisted opportunities with a readable investment dossier that names at least one actionable next validation step.
  • Decision traceability: percentage of material claims/figures that resolve to anchored source evidence or explicitly labeled assumptions; target 100% classification, not 100% factual certainty.
  • Discovery coverage: different types of demand/trade/competition signals investigated per approved radar mission; coverage is reported, not assumed.
  • Evidence sufficiency: required dimensions satisfied at agreed quality threshold before a "decision-ready" designation.
  • Research unit economics: usable evidence-backed findings per paid rupee, repeated-search rate, cached-source reuse and actual vs reserved cost.
  • Human control: zero unapproved spend, external outreach, scope expansion, or hidden background research.

1.2 Business scope

  • Geography: headquartered/operated in India; markets can include India and explicit export destinations. Store market and jurisdiction separately.
  • Industry: all legal sectors initially, subject to user restrictions and safety/legal policy. IT/cybersecurity, services, manufacturing, import/export, B2B/B2C are examples, not global boundaries.
  • Time horizons: viable to launch within 12–24 months; independent horizon for adoption/trend maturation up to 2029–2032 or user-set dates. Never treat 2032 demand as 2027 revenue.
  • Capital: user undecided; use configurable comparison bands (illustrative <₹5L, ₹5–25L, ₹25L–1Cr, >₹1Cr, unknown), not a universal eligibility filter. Every numerical market estimate must note date, geography and uncertainty.
  • Manufacturing preference: low initial capex and long shelf life as soft preferences; shelf life excludes neither technology obsolescence nor inventory exposure. Don't force this preference onto pure services.
  • Research depth: SIGNAL, SCREEN, DEEP_DIVE, MONITOR with distinct outputs and unit-cost caps.

1.3 Explicit non-goals for MVP

The system does not autonomously contact vendors/customers, conduct calls or surveys, submit grants, buy data beyond approval, place trades, register entities, make purchases, obtain licenses, file customs records, operate active penetration tests, scrape prohibited sources or certify professional legal/accounting advice. It can recommend these actions as founder-led validation tasks.

4. Detailed end-to-end journeys

J-01: Blind/global opportunity discovery

  1. Founder: "Find businesses likely to become attractive in India over the next few years, across industries; flag low-investment manufacturing and exports." System proposes domain-agnostic radar mission, asks whether any legal exclusions and budget cap exist. If unanswered, budget cap for research, not venture capital, remains a proposal requiring owner authorization.
  2. Planner chooses broad signal discovery rather than full deep-dive for every industry; sources might include reputable sector reports, dated trade data, regulatory changes, consumer adoption, competitor openings, investment activity, procurement/tender patterns, patent/product launches, and distribution constraints.
  3. Owner previews 10–20 signal queries/topics, cost envelope and exclusions; grants.
  4. Radar extracts multiple candidate business models, not generic topics like "wellness", with target buyer and revenue logic.
  5. Candidates ranked provisionally with uncertainty and alternative hypotheses, selected by owner for deeper investigation.
  6. Report explicitly separates current India adoption from projected 2029–2032 maturation.

J-02: Indian manufacturing opportunity

Founder asks low-capital, high-shelf-life industrial goods, potentially exportable. The system evaluates buyer segments, competing domestic products, import substitution evidence, HS/HSN uncertainty, bill of materials, machines and minimum efficient scale, factory premises, energy, tooling, QC, certification, supplier availability, throughput, defective yield, shelf life, channel margins, inventory, shipping, FX, government incentives. It cannot assert supplier quotation or government award if only a public estimate exists. Produces an investment range, cash-conversion cycle, production ramp and field-validation plan.

J-03: Cybersecurity services opportunity

System evaluates business-buyer categories and demand, skills/credibility/certifications, differentiation, legal scope, cyber insurance/liability, tool cost, service delivery workload, sales cycle, price benchmarks and client acquisition. Offensive testing is out of scope: research is legal/market intelligence, not penetration operations. Team-fit benefits IT background but requires honest gap analysis.

J-04: Deep dive from shortlist

Owner selects an opportunity and sets research depth, export destinations, preferred capital band and quality threshold. The mission reuses source-backed prior claims where valid, gathers missing data, calculates scenarios and produces structured dossier. If important numbers cannot be sourced, report missingness and owner-approved assumptions.

J-05: Group discussion and mission revision

In Slack #venture-research, Founder A creates mission in thread. B suggests B2B, C asks for EU exports. Only explicit @ResearchBot/question-action/approved summary gets processed. Conversation service proposes a diff, planner assesses incremental work and spend, owner approves exact v2 plan, affected tasks restart or branch without losing completed v1 records. No passive channel surveillance.

J-06: Clarification during research

An agent sees machinery estimates that differ materially; creates structured CLARIFICATION_REQUESTED with affected tasks, reason, options, suggested safe default, deadline, and expected incremental cost. Task branch pauses; unrelated approved tasks continue. A founder answer either resolves a local assumption or creates a versioned new mission proposal when material.

J-07: No-availability/weak research

If a public source is unavailable, contradictory, outdated or legally restricted: system records attempted query and reason, does not invent data, optionally selects alternate permitted sources inside plan, then finishes with INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, PARTIAL or field validation advice. No indefinite follow-up loop.

J-08: Monitoring and weekly report

Founder creates a separately approved recurring radar or watchlist monitor, with cadence, run cap, monthly cap, stop date and notifications. The service checks source changes, refreshes affected claims and summarizes changes, not rerunning the entire historical dossier. If recurring authorization expires or the global cap would be breached, it pauses and asks for renewal. No schedule runs without approval.

J-09: Knowledge challenge and audit

Founder clicks ROI estimate. UI shows formula, all inputs, source link/extract/date, currency conversion, uncertainty, calculated sensitivity and decision history. Founder marks source disputed; dependent findings become NEEDS_REVIEW until revalidated. Audit includes who decided and which version.

J-10: Local-to-AWS deployment

Founders complete mock and local live testing on Windows Docker. Authorized operator creates AWS resources after credit eligibility and cost checks, restores database and artifact hashes to cloud, verifies single active Slack bot and switches only after backup/rollback readiness. App logic remains identical.

02 — Research Methodology, Agent Registry and Bounded Planning

1. Philosophy

VRS is not a committee of LLM personas exchanging chat forever. It is a research production system: a mission authorizes a plan; a deterministic scheduler runs typed tasks implemented by reusable agent capabilities; the evidence service stores atomic outputs; a verifier checks their quality; a calculator computes economics; a synthesis agent renders a dossier; the founder decides. The system's capability to create/spawn agents means instantiating registered tasks or temporary specialist configurations, not compiling/executing new AI-written Python or accepting unvetted MCP tools.

2. End-to-end stage workflow

  1. Discover: scan agreed markets/signals in controlled batches. Result: opportunity hypotheses and source-linked signal records.
  2. Normalize: merge duplicated candidates, classify sector/customer/problem/business model, preserve provenance and rejected hypotheses.
  3. Screen: low-cost viability with explicit unknowns; capital band, competition, demand signals, target customer, launch effort, legal risks.
  4. Select: founder chooses one or more candidates. No automatic paid deep dive without separate approval.
  5. Deep research: tailored dimensions based on opportunity type and market; gather primary public data and reputable secondary research.
  6. Challenge: independent verification, alternate hypotheses and negative evidence; bounded rework on crucial gaps.
  7. Quantify: calculations in code, scenarios and currency/unit normalization.
  8. Assess: market attractiveness + team execution fit + evidence grade + hard-stop list.
  9. Decide: founder accepts watch/validate/reject; the AI may recommend but cannot commit capital.
  10. Monitor/refresh: separately scheduled, cost-bounded, source-specific change checks.

Stage transitions have policy gates and a source-coverage report. A run that stops at cap can still be validly PARTIAL, not COMPLETED.

3. Research strategy: find opportunities, not buzzwords

3.2 Discovery query planning

Planner composes a balanced search portfolio from approved signals. Each query has: question ID, market/sector, source preference, target freshness, search provider, max results, a reason why the query adds information, and the number of planned/actual calls. It should prevent duplicate semantic queries and stop redundant searches when evidence coverage stops improving. Broad scans are sampled across categories, not exhaustive web scraping.

3.3 Candidate generation

A discovery agent proposes specific business options: e.g., "manufacture replacement consumable part P for B2B segment Q in state R" as a hypothesis; do not write that example as a real validated opportunity. Each candidate requires source-linked market signal and customer problem. Mark HYPOTHESIS until screening. Separate drivers, current signals, projected dates and actionable business model. Preserve alternate suggestions and reject duplicates.

3.4 Opportunity scoring for radar

Radar relevance is initially qualitative (strong/moderate/weak/unsupported) based on source diversity, India applicability, accessible customer segment, launch horizon and testability. It must not automatically extrapolate web mentions into market size. Ranking is provisional and founders can override priority with recorded rationale.

7. Planning engine detailed algorithm

Inputs: immutable mission version; opportunity/candidate; existing knowledge; stage; registry; organizational security policy; tool price catalog; global quotas; owner preferences.

  1. Validate mission object; identify critical unknowns (launch country, intended product nature, business customer). Ask high-impact questions, not every optional field.
  2. Build question coverage matrix from stage: universal demand/competition/financial/risk, plus manufacturing/trade/export/cyber domain-specific. Mark N/A explicitly with rationale.
  3. Retrieve relevant recent evidence by subject, geography and observation period. Build reuse vs refresh candidates; permission and licensing checked.
  4. Candidate planner proposes task intents and capabilities. Require registered manifest references. Use small LLM tier or deterministic template to minimize spend.
  5. Normalize duplicate questions, merge search work, choose smallest covering capability set and respect task count cap. If incomplete coverage, propose cheaper plan with warning, never silently omit a mandatory dimension.
  6. Build DAG. Examples: discovery→screening; manufacturing/supply/competitor/demand→verification; financial input collection→financial code model; verification+finance+risk→final assessment. Some verification runs may proceed in parallel before final synthesis.
  7. Assign per-task estimated token/search/fetch budgets and wall time; reserve plan maximum, and ensure per-task maxima do not sum to more than authorized envelope without an explicit shared cap policy.
  8. Validate graph acyclic, manifest versions exist, tool permissions compatible, no duplicate task IDs, no unresolved dependencies, no unsupported jurisdiction, data source constraints, or arbitrary code.
  9. Generate human-readable plan explaining relevance of each specialist, expected research, reuse, gaps, sources, time, estimate+max cost, quality thresholds and failure behavior.
  10. Owner approves exact canonical mission+plan hashes. After approval, runtime receives StartRun(grant_id); no unapproved deep fetch before this.

8. Spawn/delegation semantics

  • Specialists may emit MORE_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED or SPECIALIST_TASK_PROPOSED with reason, exact missing question, expected info gain, requested capability, tool class and estimated cost.
  • Orchestrator checks (i) a suitable active/finished task already exists, (ii) approved parent plan allows optional followup class, (iii) depth and cumulative task cap, (iv) remaining budget/search/tool limits, (v) dependency cycle and source safety, (vi) the task's impact on mission/plan hash and approval.
  • If followup was not explicitly within the approved task envelope, it becomes a plan revision and waits for owner approval. It cannot hide inside a single generic approval.
  • Child tasks do not recursively create limitless children: maximum depth, per-parent proposals, task counts, wall time, and maximum verification repair passes are hard coded/configurable.
  • An AgentInstance is an ephemeral execution record (task ID + manifest ref + state) with no privileged independent tool credentials.

9. Scheduler, idempotency, stop conditions

9.1 Scheduler

  • A persistent queue stores runnable tasks. Dependency-complete work is READY; transactional row leases assign to worker(s), with heartbeat and reclaimer.
  • Concurrency cap per mission and globally. One logical task has at most one live lease. Duplicate queue delivery results in same task and no repeated side effect.
  • Per operation: validate grant/policy, verify status+deadline, reserve maximal charge and global quotas atomically, execute through tool gateway, record event/usage/claim refs and settle cost. Uncertain network result enters UNKNOWN_OUTCOME pending reconciliation.

9.2 Stop predicate, not "LLM decides done"

A task ends at first applicable: (a) mandatory questions covered at threshold, (b) impossible/no permissible source, (c) budget/quota exhausted, (d) maximum calls/retries/depth reached, (e) runtime deadline, (f) owner stop, (g) blocking question, (h) fatal validation/security breach. Terminal result includes stop_reason and per-question coverage.

A mission is decision-ready only if: all applicable mandatory dimensions are SUPPORTED (or founder-approved explicit risk acceptance), critical numbers source-backed or named assumptions, contradictions resolved or displayed, financial math validated, verification complete, hard-stop risks surfaced and no pending mandatory owner action. Otherwise PARTIAL / VALIDATION_REQUIRED / INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.

9.3 Bounded refinement

A verifier may request one configurable remediation pass on a claim within remaining approved allocation; after failure, persist gap and stop. Do not force false confidence just to pass completeness. Repeating same query with different wording without new rationale is not valid progress.

9.4 Repeated/stuck detection

Record canonical query hashes, information-gain estimate, repeated tool errors and last N state signatures. If no net new verifiable evidence after configured N attempts or state repeats, stop NO_PROGRESS and notify founder. This works even if LLM attempts to continue.

10. Agent-to-agent information exchange

  • No raw large chat transcripts passed between specialists. Task inputs carry domain questions, validated facts/claim IDs, assumptions and controlled context snippets.
  • Source extraction → structured EvidenceClaim → verified Finding → deterministic calculator inputs → score/report. Agents can query Knowledge Hub by ID and filter.
  • Each event has event_id, mission_id/version/hash, run_id, task_id, correlation_id, causation_id, idempotency_key, type, timestamp and schema-versioned payload.
  • Events emitted only after state commit; worker consumes idempotently. External endpoints cannot trigger new work solely from untrusted retrieved content.

11. Research quality control

  • Material claim: sourced, dated, observation period and jurisdiction identified, or explicitly marked estimate/assumption/forecast/unknown.
  • Cross-source corroboration when available; independence matters (two sites republishing same press release are not two sources).
  • For high-impact numbers (capital, subsidy, tariff, market size), prefer original official/filing/credible primary source; mark unverifiable estimates and professional validation need.
  • Negative evidence is compulsory before PROMISING: direct or indirect competitor strength, regulatory gate, acquisition channel cost, financing risks, cash conversion and obsolescence.
  • When sources conflict, preserve both versions with dates and resolution state rather than selecting convenient one.
  • Always list research coverage and overlooked areas caused by source limitations or constrained spend.

12. Cost-efficiency algorithms

  • Cache normalized search results (policy-appropriate TTL), fetched allowed extracts keyed by URL/date/content hash, reusable claims with context tags. Cache access still passes permission/license checks.
  • Route planning/classification/extraction through economical models with structured output; choose stronger model for conflicting evidence and synthesis only when quality requires it.
  • Use bulk per-page extraction rather than one LLM per sentence; context windows contain selected relevant evidence IDs/extracts, not raw complete research histories.
  • Early-stop weak opportunities after screening and require founder shortlist approval for expensive deep research.
  • Track marginal evidence gain per request, repeated searches and wasted token ratio on dashboards.

13. Research coverage and release test examples

  • In mock mode two candidates: physical product and service. Planner selects manufacturing for one, cybersecurity (if relevant) for the other; common demand/competition/risk used in both.

  • Verification detects conflicting machine cost: source discrepancy flagged and financial scenario sensitivity shown, without inventing resolved value.
  • Stop/restart/duplicate event cases do not double-spend.

4. Conversation Manager specification

4.1 Inputs

Authenticated Slack/user command or dashboard chat; current mission state and version; question state; accessible existing profile/preferences; relevant mission messages only where authorized.

4.2 Parsing

It detects intent: CREATE_MISSION, ANSWER_QUESTION, PROPOSE_CHANGE, ASK_REPORT, CHALLENGE_CLAIM, REQUEST_PLAN, REQUEST_RUN_STATUS, REQUEST_PAUSE, REQUEST_STOP, START_MONITOR, REQUEST_THREAD_SUMMARY, UNRELATED.

VRS collects sources and atomic, falsifiable claims instead of just storing long reports in a vector store. Never simply overwrite a contradictory claim.

source → extract → claim → finding → report section → score/input → human decision. Direction can be reversed from the dashboard. 2. Evidence verification policy

  • Provenance: every substantive figure must have source or assumption ID, publication/observation/retrieval dates, geo and unit.
  • Authority: for government subsidy eligibility, current legal status, tariff or regulatory rule, prioritize official agency policy/notification. Secondary summaries are discovery aids, not sufficient final proof.
  • Independence: 10 copied blog posts using one original report count as one evidence lineage, not 10 independent confirmations.
  • Recency by domain: configurable: fast-moving prices and deadlines require frequent check; multi-year demographics/trade series may legitimately lag. Display exact source series end period. No universal 30-day rule.
  • Contradiction: include conflicting source assertions (often due to date, geography, currency or methodology), suggest reconciliation, flag affected outputs; may use ranges and sensitivity rather than resolve falsely.
  • Quality grade: A robust applicable primary/recent corroborated; B reasonable with limits; C weak/single-source; D speculation; INSUFFICIENT critical coverage absent. It is not a mathematical probability that a claim is true. Sources and claims can receive different grades.
  • Stale evidence: freshness depends on source type and claim risk. A source cited in a new mission keeps original observed date and marks stale until rechecked. Avoid silently treating the latest retrieved article as the latest underlying data.
  • Adversarial source: input is untrusted; page text instructing agent to reveal secrets or change mission is ignored and flagged.
  • Unsupported numbers: no specific investment, market size, subsidy, tariffs or pricing invented by LLM.

3. Knowledge store / retrieval

3.1 Taxonomy

Opportunity, TrendSignal, CustomerSegment, Industry, Product, Service, Competitor, Substitute, Supplier, BusinessModel, Market, Country, State, TradeCode, Regulation, GovernmentScheme, FinanceModel, Assumption, Source, Extract, Claim, Finding, Dossier, Decision, Monitor, ResearchMission, Task, ToolCall.

3.2 Retrieval mechanics

Use SQL structured lookup (entity, jurisdiction, dates and relevance tags), PostgreSQL full-text for excerpts, then optional vector similarity after measured benefit. Prefer structured applicability gates over semantic similarity: a Tamil Nadu manufacturing incentive isn't automatically valid for a Karnataka service startup; a 2024 grant offer isn't necessarily active now. Returned knowledge carries source and limitation metadata, never just a naked paragraph.

3.3 Reuse + refresh

For each candidate claim: (a) user permissions and source license, (b) exact subject/product alignment, (c) location/industry eligibility, (d) observed/effective date, (e) independent status, (f) contradiction, (g) dependency impact. Decision: REUSE_VERIFIED, REUSE_WITH_WARNING, REFRESH_AUTHORIZED, REJECT_FOR_CONTEXT. Refresh requires a grant or authorized recurring budget.

3.4 Content lifecycle

Store metadata and permitted extracts, deduplicate by canonical URL/content hash and acquisition version; apply retention; support deletion where legally required without falsifying historical audit (retained redacted tombstone). Do not assume unrestricted copying of paywalled/commercial datasets. Record data source Terms of Use, API limits and extraction permission.

5. Government funding, subsidy and incentive module

5.1 Why this is an independent research discipline

A published central/state scheme may be expired, allocated, only available to an incorporated/DPIIT recognized startup, incubator-gated, tied to project location, industry or expenditure, restricted to women/youth/rural business, or offered only as credit guarantee rather than a cash grant. Recommendations cannot just list names and advertised maxima.

5.2 GovernmentScheme record

Required fields:

  • scheme_id, name, implementing_authority, central_or_state, state, sector, purpose, official_url, notification_id, document_version, valid_from, valid_until.
  • support_type: NON_REPAYABLE_GRANT, DEBT, CREDIT_GUARANTEE, EQUITY_OR_CONVERTIBLE, TAX_REBATE, CAPITAL_SUBSIDY, INTEREST_SUBVENTION, LAND_OR_INFRA, TRAINING, INCUBATION, PROCUREMENT_ACCESS, OTHER.
  • benefit_formula, published_maximum, cofunding_pct, eligible_expense_categories, reimbursement_vs_advance, milestone_conditions, disbursement_conditions, repayment_obligations, tax_implications_to_review.
  • eligibility: incorporated form, MSME/Udyam, DPIIT recognition, enterprise age, turnover, employment, sector, location, founder category only when voluntarily provided, technology maturity, incubator and financial criteria.
  • application_open_date, application_close_date, portal_status: OPEN|CLOSED|ANNOUNCED|PAUSED|UNKNOWN; status_checked_at, source_evidence_ids.
  • fit_status: LIKELY_ELIGIBLE|LIKELY_INELIGIBLE|REQUIRES_DETAILS|UNKNOWN, missing facts, next proof, authority to contact, review notes.

5.3 Scheme agent algorithm

  1. Discover candidate schemes based on industry, business model, India state, likely legal vehicle and capital plan.
  2. Fetch official latest notification/guideline and separate it from press or secondary summaries.
  3. Extract formula and eligibility conditions with anchored evidence and validity dates. Check whether application is currently open independently from benefit being published.
  4. Compare founders' known information; missing incorporation or location yields REQUIRES_DETAILS, not false eligibility.
  5. Distinguish promised vs accessible cash timing. For reimbursement schemes, financial model must account for paying initial spend upfront and delayed claims.
  6. Check incompatibilities, matching contributions, ceilings, double-benefit restrictions and potential funding delay. Flag professional verification.
  7. Report benefit as POTENTIAL only; no subsidy becomes guaranteed cash in base-case projection unless separately verified as approved and collectible.
  8. Recommend application documentation and founder-led verification steps; never submit application automatically.

5.4 Example report style (illustrative)

"Scheme S could offer a capital reimbursement conditional on location, eligible machinery and approved enterprise status. Application status unknown as of last official check; do not book it as received financing. Need incorporation proof, location, purchase invoices, current notification and scheme officer confirmation." No invented amount.

6. Manufacturing module

6.1 Core fields and calculations

  • product_specification, use and category, customer segment, shelf life (physical/chemical), storage sensitivity, warranty/repair, obsolescence, seasonality, regulatory standards and quality regime.
  • Make vs buy/contract manufacture; machinery/tooling cost and depreciation assumptions, installation/commissioning, facility and utility requirements, environmental and labor compliance.
  • Raw material sources, BOM items, variable consumption, MOQ, supplier concentration, import dependence, alternate sources, lead times, shipping and FX.
  • Capacity units/hour, utilization ramp, batch yield/reject, QC cost, downtime, minimum viable run, lead time and labor requirements.
  • Packaging, warehousing, shrinkage/spoilage, shelf life vs inventory residence, obsolete finished goods, cash tied up in raw and finished inventory.
  • Certification/test cost and time, production trial milestones, distribution channel margin, returns and replacement.

6.2 Mandatory distinctions

Physical shelf life ≠ commercial sellability; long-lived hardware may become technologically obsolete, tied to specific machines, or fail evolving standards. Low machine cost ≠ low total investment; working capital, spare tooling, minimum raw material order and delayed B2B collections can dominate. Separate factory setup from outsourced production scenarios whenever plausible.

6.3 Manufacturing financial inputs

setup_capex, machine_capex, tooling, installation, facility_deposit, inventory_raw, inventory_WIP, inventory_finished, receivables, trade_payables, quality_cost, scrap_rate, yield, capacity, utilization, labor, utilities, freight, channel_discount, warranty, taxes_and_duties. 7. Export, trading and import-substitution module

  • Country-level demand: import volumes/values and product category, latest year/month actually observed, major supplying countries, domestic vs foreign competition and market access.
  • HS/HSN classification candidate with source and expert-confirmation warning; duties, standards, labeling, sanctions/export-control checks where relevant.
  • India legal prerequisites (e.g. import/export registration and other product-specific licenses) are checked from current official sources by product and transaction type.
  • Export economics: ex-factory price, packing, inland freight, shipping mode, insurance, customs/clearance, duties/taxes by Incoterm, FX, returns, trade finance, payment delays, distribution commission, after-sales support and local compliance.
  • Working capital due to export transit/credit; country, buyer and currency concentration risk.
  • Import substitution analysis must compare full landed imported cost and quality/availability with proposed Indian manufacture, not just CIF import price.
  • Make export opportunity outputs destination-specific; do not generalize EU to all member-specific requirements without citing scope.

8. Services / IT / cybersecurity module

  • Customer buyer personas, contract types, margins and utilization, repeat business vs projects, sales cycle, customer acquisition and retention, service-level expectations, liability and privacy/cyber regulation.
  • Skills matrix: expertise founders already possess vs training/hiring/partnering; certifications, industry references, security compliance, software/tool licensing and operational/on-call needs.
  • For cybersecurity: allowed research is market and service design only; prohibit intrusive scanning, credential probing, exploitation or intelligence gathering that targets unauthorized organizations.

9. Financial model engine

9.1 Philosophy

LLM extracts candidate inputs and explains them; versioned deterministic functions calculate financial results. Outputs have uncertainty bands and documented formulas, not false exactness. Separate business economic investment from VRS research operating cost.

9.2 Units, time, exchange

Each input: amount, currency, unit, basis (per-unit/month/year/once), price_date, exchange_rate_id if converted, source_ids, confidence, low/base/high, and tax inclusion. INR is default reporting currency but retain source currency and the rate/time used; explain inflation and nominal vs real assumptions.

9.3 Startup investment model

InitialFundingNeeded = OneTimeCAPEX + Setup/Compliance/Deposits + InitialWorkingCapital + PreRevenueOperatingBurn + Contingency - ConfirmedUsableExternalFinancing (avoid double-counting inventory, deposits and subsidy reimbursements). Show maximum cumulative cash deficit from month-by-month forecast as a more informative financing estimate where modeled.

WorkingCapital = Inventory + TradeReceivables + OtherOperatingCurrentAssets - TradePayables - OtherOperatingCurrentLiabilities with assumptions about DSO/DIO/DPO and operating cycle; not all fixed asset cash outlay belongs in working capital.

UnitContribution = NetRealizedSellingPrice - UnitVariableProductionCost - UnitVariableFulfillment/ChannelCost - VariableReturnsAndWarranty.

MonthlyOperatingContribution = UnitsSold × UnitContribution - MonthlyFixedOperatingCosts.

AccountingOperatingProfit and cash flow must be tracked separately. Do not confuse depreciation, principal repayments, taxes and cash payments.

SimpleBreakEvenUnits = FixedMonthlyCosts / PositiveUnitContribution, only if unit contribution > 0 and steady-state assumptions apply; otherwise no finite simple break-even.

Payback requires positive cumulative cash recovery and clearly states whether calculated with financing/taxes and inflation; do not give payback if not recovered within forecast.

9.4 Scenario and sensitivity inputs

Minimum PESSIMISTIC, BASE, OPTIMISTIC, each with independently adjustable volume ramp, realized price, raw material cost, yield, sales cycle, customer acquisition, financing, subsidy cash timing, FX and compliance delays. Compare cash needed at month 0, 3, 6, 12, 18, 24; break-even time, downside exposure and bottleneck. Optionally stress-test no subsidy, 30% demand shortfall, longer receivables or extra regulation—test inputs are hypothetical.

9.5 Financial quality controls

  • Prevent circular references, impossible negative quantities, inconsistent time bases, double-counted CAPEX/working capital, tax assumption inconsistencies and undocumented exchange rates.
  • If essential price or costs unknown, produce bounds or INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, not a fabricated "expected ROI".
  • Model assumptions must be editable by founders with reason and immutable revision history; recalculation is deterministic and testable.
  • Any external subsidy classed as POTENTIAL appears only in optional scenario; not in assured base cash unless formally validated.

10. Market attractiveness, team-fit and evidence scoring

10.1 Separate dimensions

Market Attractiveness (0–100 only with enough data): demand/current trend, WTP/pricing proxies, competitive gap, profitability potential, defensibility, scalability, risk and timing. Team Execution Fit: affordability, skills gap/time to upskill, implementation duration, sourcing and distribution reach, complexity and founder preference. Evidence Quality: A–D / INSUFFICIENT based on source age, independence, coverage and contradiction. Hard stops: illegal/inaccessible market, mandatory certification impossible in target window, unacceptable safety/liability or cash constraint; never averaged away.

10.2 Configurable scoring

Weights are editable and versioned per research profile. Missing high-impact factors yield NOT_ENOUGH_EVIDENCE or an explicitly provisional range—not automatically 50/100. Display per-factor evidence source, judgment basis and influence. Long shelf life is scored in applicable physical-good models; services are N/A, not punished.

10.3 Independent challenge

The red-team verifier must produce the strongest countercase: reasons market demand could be illusory; incumbent advantage; capital / FX / inventory surprises; regulation or subsidy ineligibility; team capability limits; risk of obsolete product; why current competitors may already have saturated demand. It must not invent negatives either. Final dossier includes "Reasons NOT to pursue".

11. Primary market validation plan (no outbound actions)

When evidence insufficient or high-stakes: recommend who to interview (customer, distributor, exporter, supplier, regulator, accountant), purpose, exact open questions, minimum number/data quality target as planning suggestion not scientific universal, expected cost/time, success threshold and owner. Example: supplier quotes for 3 comparable machine specifications and validation of warranty/installation, customer purchase frequency from interviews, and compliance document confirmation with agency. System cannot directly contact them without a future separately designed opt-in feature.

12. Source-based refresh and monitoring

A monitored change identifies claim IDs affected by a newly released trade series, price list, policy status or competitor announcement. Report delta, economic sensitivity and changed recommendation, with source observation period. A scheduled refresh follows an approved subscription with per-run and cumulative cost caps; it may not autonomously deepen into unrelated research.

13. Output QA checklist

Before presenting an investment dossier, validate: sections 1–15 of PRS; dates/markets/source IDs on all material numbers; two-axis score/evidence flags; no unverified subsidy booked as cash; negative evidence; launch 12–24 months; cash requirements; skills; manufacturing special topics when applicable; exports per country; explicit unknowns; field validation; risk hard stops; cost and scope coverage; immutability of report snapshot.

8. Event-driven reliability and database transactions

  • Inbound Slack: verify signature/Socket Mode envelope, persist unique event ID in inbox, acknowledge promptly, enqueue command in transaction, then process asynchronously. Duplicate delivery must be logically once.
  • Outbox: write business state change and event in one DB transaction; worker publishes outbox with retries. Event consumers idempotent. No direct send to Slack followed by uncertain DB write when consistency matters.
  • Queue: SELECT ... FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED, lease owner+expiry, heartbeat, concurrency limits, dead-letter failure after retry cap. DB restart recovery tests required.
  • Checkpoints: workflow engine state is a resumability aid; PostgreSQL business tables remain authoritative for grant, mission, cost and evidence. An interrupt may re-enter node logic; external effects need idempotent wrapper.
  • External APIs: write tool_call(INTENDED) and reserve max cost before dispatch. Reconcile confirmed request ID/usage. Unknown outcomes not blindly retried. If provider supports idempotency key, use it; else mark UNKNOWN_OUTCOME and require reconciliation/owner-approved retry.
  • Cancellation: set durable cancel flag; before each tool/model/search call, policy checks flag. In-flight requests cannot always be undone; account for uncertainty.

9. Budget Guard design

9.1 Hard limits

Per run: total chargeable cost in accounting currency, model input/output token caps, search/fetch request count, total tasks, parallelism, depth, per-task retries, wall time, maximum unanswered questions and provider-specific rates. Global workspace monthly cap and per-provider cap apply independently.

9.2 Atomic reservation

Pseudocode:

@transactional
async def authorize_and_reserve(grant_id, tool_class, max_possible_charge, expected_usage, idempotency_key):
    grant = lock_grant_row(grant_id)
    assert grant.active and grant.matches_current_run_plan()
    assert tool_class in grant.allowed_tools
    assert no_cancel_flag() and within_time_and_resource_limits()
    assert usage.actual + usage.reserved + max_possible_charge <= grant.hard_cap
    assert workspace_usage + max_possible_charge <= workspace_global_cap
    return create_unique_reserved_tool_call(idempotency_key, max_possible_charge)

Settle actual usage from provider-reported counters; difference refunds reservation. If response/usage unknown, keep UNCERTAIN amount reserved until reconciled. Use fixed-point integer micro-currency for ledger; record price catalog version and exchange rate/time. Account for fee types (input/cache/output/search/fetch/infrastructure) distinctly. Provider alerts are secondary; a tool API may not provide hard cost caps.

9.3 Important caveats

A provider may bill a call even if user cancels it, or use variable search prices. Worst-case reservation must include maximum response tokens and provider rate; estimates not guarantees. Display total authorized cap separately from expected spend. Reconcile external invoices periodically when possible.

6. Budget/approval protocol

  • APPROVAL_REQUESTED must persist human-readable summary snapshot alongside exact hashes, expected lower/high spend, maximum absolute spend and configured currency, tool classes and expiry. Owner cannot unknowingly approve a different payload.
  • APPROVAL_GRANTED results from role+nonce+hash+expiry+global policy checks; grant_id immutable. If mission/plan changed, reject and reissue.
  • Worker authorize_and_reserve() locks grant budget row and global budget row atomically, checks sum of actual+reserved+uncertain and remaining quotas, reserves maximum call amount; only then returns an opaque operation capability token scoped to a single call.
  • Tool gateway uses that authorization token, records returned provider request ID, usage, result status, hashes and costs; prevents agents from calling underlying provider outside gateway.
  • Settlement reconciles actual and refunds remaining reservation. Unknown request results remain UNCERTAIN until reconciled. Retry with same key never duplicates a chargeable attempt automatically.
  • Chargeable planning during live mode needs pre-approved planning allowance or no chargeable planning; the system cannot spend before authorizing the plan being planned. Initial draft parse can use a small admin-approved operational budget with caps and disclosure; G0/G1 mock only.

7. Task DAG and completion contract

  • dependencies must reference plan-local IDs; no circular graph; each active task's manifest version in plan registry.
  • ready(task) iff all required dependencies completed/satisfied according to failure policy and no blocking question; incomplete prerequisite can yield partial output or blocked downstream with recorded reason.
  • If important evidence contradiction occurs, verifier generates bounded remediation suggestion. Orchestrator checks approved caps and plan; otherwise stop with discrepancy.
  • Run complete when all tasks terminal, no pending mandatory approvals, evidence coverage calculated; COMPLETED is distinct from dossier DECISION_READY.

8. Data quality and schema evolution

  • Migrations via Alembic with test database; no destructive migration on cloud without backup and explicit review.
  • Schema versions declared in payloads and migration strategy. Unknown newer event version cannot silently process with older handler; dead letter + notify.
  • Claim/value types and numeric precision tested using fixtures and property-based unit tests (e.g., break-even and cash balance equations).
  • Index candidate: missions by workspace/owner/status; task queue state/next run; grant expiration; claims by subject+geo+observed; source canonical URL+hash; full-text materialized index where justified.
  • Retention/soft-delete of discussion extracts and sources must not break audit references: use redacted/tombstone metadata, no hidden data leakage.

9. External integration contract and tests

  • Slack gateway persists team_id, event_id, channel, thread_ts, verified user_id, action_id, trigger_source. Reject self-loop bot events. Use one active listener per workspace at a time.
  • Search connector carries result title/snippet URL and retrieval metadata, not a guarantee of factual accuracy. Fetcher enforces source policy and SSRF protections independently of search result.
  • Source connector errors are typed: ACCESS_DENIED, RATE_LIMIT, NOT_FOUND, CHANGED, UNSAFE_URL, LICENSE_DENIED, CONTENT_TOO_LARGE, PARSING_FAILED, TIMEOUT and UNKNOWN.
  • LLM adapter schema invalid response is quarantined/repaired within budget and max retries; no raw string executed or trusted as permission.
  • Finance API only takes validated typed input and calculation version; outputs reproducible after restart.

16 / Quick definitions

Glossary & interpretation guide

Use these definitions when explaining the platform to founders, developers, investors and reviewers so product, research and technical concepts are not confused.

RMS / Mission

Versioned declaration of intended research scope, preferences, limits and outputs.

Research plan

Approved task graph describing how the system will investigate the mission.

Owner grant

Authenticated exact-hash approval authorizing specific work and maximum resources.

Capability manifest

Versioned definition of a specialist agent’s applicability, inputs, outputs and tools.

Agent task

One bounded unit of specialist research associated with a registered manifest.

DAG

Directed acyclic task graph used to express dependencies without cycles.

Clarification

Question asked by the system when input is missing or evidence is materially ambiguous.

Blocking question

Question that pauses affected task branches until resolved or timed out.

Source

External document, dataset, official page or permitted file with provenance metadata.

Extract

Specific anchored section or data excerpt from a source.

Atomic claim

Falsifiable fact/estimate linked to evidence, time and geography.

Finding

Synthesized conclusion supported by explicit claims and uncertainty.

Observation period

When underlying data were measured; distinct from article publication date.

Evidence coverage

Degree to which all required research dimensions have supported/unknown conclusions.

Radar

Broad proactive search and screening of emerging business opportunities.

Dossier

Structured full investment research report, source-traceable and versioned.

TAM / SAM / SOM

Total, serviceable and realistically obtainable markets; only estimate when data permit.

CAPEX / OPEX

Capital expenditure vs ongoing operational expense.

Working capital

Cash absorbed by stock, receivables and operating timing net of payables.

WTP

Willingness to pay. Price listings are proxies, not direct evidence of customer commitment.

Shelf life

Physical/chemical usability; distinct from obsolescence and the cash cost of inventory.

Landed cost

Full delivered import/export cost including freight, duties, fees and related assumptions.

HS code

Trade commodity classification; may require professional verification for a particular SKU.

Subsidy realization

Actual receipt of an incentive, which may depend on eligibility, deadlines and reimbursements.

Idempotency

Guaranteeing a retried request does not cause duplicate logical actions.

Outbox / inbox

Durable queues of outgoing/incoming events to prevent lost or duplicated logical processing.

Checkpoint

Persisted workflow position enabling pause/resume and recovery.

SSRF

Server-side request forgery risk when software fetches untrusted URLs.

RPO / RTO

Recovery point and recovery time objectives for data loss and service restoration.
